Why do we talk about "Localization" and not just "Translation"?

Localization is the process of culturally and functionally adapting a digital product for a specific market. We don't just change the language, but also take care of details like:

  • Formats of dates, times and units of measurement.
  • Local currencies and preferred payment gateways.
  • Cultural nuances, idioms and colloquial expressions.
  • Adaptation of web design (ensuring the translated text fits perfectly in the interface).

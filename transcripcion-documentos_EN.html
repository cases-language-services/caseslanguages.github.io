📝 Transcription of Audio and Video

We turn your audio and video files into accurate, editable written texts with the format you need. Professional solutions for corporate, legal, research, and media environments.

Our Transcription Solutions

⚖️ Legal and Judicial Transcriptions

Exact conversion of hearings, trials, witness statements, security recordings, or phone calls. We deliver ready and structured formats to be presented as evidence in legal proceedings.

🏢 Corporate Transcriptions

We record board meetings, shareholder meetings, press conferences, webinars, market research interviews, and important corporate speeches in writing.

🎬 Media and Subtitle Transcription

We transcribe audiovisual material (podcasts, YouTube videos, documentaries, movies, online courses) with timestamps ready for translation, localization, or subtitling processes.

How do you prefer your transcriptions?

We adapt our work according to your specific requirements for fidelity and purpose:

  • Literal Transcription (Verbatim): Includes every recorded word exactly, including laughter, hesitations, filler words, pauses, repetitions, and pronunciation errors (highly requested in judicial and linguistic research contexts).
  • Clean Transcription (Natural): Background noise, fillers, or unnecessary redundancies are omitted to deliver a fluent, grammatically corrected, and easy-to-read text without altering the original message at all (ideal for speeches, publications, or corporate minutes).
  • Transcription with Direct Translation: We listen to your material in the original language and transcribe it directly translated into Spanish or the language you need in one step.

Do you have files you need documented?

Upload your audio or video files to your preferred platform (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive) and send us the download link so we can analyze audio quality and provide you with an accurate estimate of time and budget.

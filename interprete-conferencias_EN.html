🎙️ Professional and Conference Interpretation

Break down language barriers in real-time. We offer highly skilled interpreters to ensure fluid, accurate, and natural communication in your live, hybrid, or virtual events in Paraguay.

Our Interpretation Modalities

🎧 Simultaneous Interpretation

Ideal for large congresses, seminars, and conferences. Interpreters work from booths, transmitting the message in real time to attendees via wireless receivers.

🗣️ Consecutive Interpretation

Perfect for bilateral meetings, press conferences, official speeches, or field visits. The speaker talks and takes pauses for the interpreter, supported by note-taking, to convey the message in the target language.

💻 Remote Video Interpretation (VRI)

We adapt our services to the digital era. We provide technical support and professional real-time interpretation for your events and virtual meetings through platforms such as Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or Webex.

Integrated Solutions for Your Event

At Cases Language Services, we not only provide the linguistic professional, but also advise you on all the logistics necessary to ensure the success of the event:

  • cutting-edge Technical Equipment: Coordination of soundproof interpretation booths, consoles, transmitters and receivers of high fidelity audio.

Our Linguistic Specialization

At Cases Language Services, we work specialized with five key languages for business, academia, diplomacy, and cultural development:

🇬🇧 English

The premier tool for global communication, international trade, and academia. We provide translation, interpretation, and training in English with the highest terminological rigor.

🇩🇪 German

A strategic language for technology, the sciences, and European business relations. Particularly relevant for handling consular procedures, study scholarships, and technical cooperation in the country.

🇨🇳 Mandarin Chinese

The key language for the economy of the future and bilateral trade. We offer technical and commercial translations that enable local and international companies to interact effectively with the Asian market.

🇵🇾 Guaraní

Our official language and invaluable cultural heritage. We provide Guaraní (Avañe'ẽ) translation, localization, and teaching services to ensure your message resonates with local identity and authenticity across the nation.

🇧🇷 Portuguese

The language of our strategic regional partner. We facilitate commercial, legal, and tourism integration within Mercosur through swift and accurate translations for the Brazilian market.

Linguistic Quality Assurance

To ensure cultural and technical accuracy in each of these five languages, we follow rigorous standards:

  • Native, Certified Translators: Professionals with native-level command and the required legal certifications for sworn translation.
  • Industry Specialization: Projects are assigned to linguists with experience in the specific field (legal, medical, commercial, etc.).
  • Respect for Local Identity: Meticulous adaptation of each project to the target market, whether local or global.
